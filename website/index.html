Atelier Standards · Fabric Engineering

Engineered to Hold Its Drape.

Most commercial streetwear dissolves after three washes. We built Shelby Hiver on architectural cotton integrity.

02 / COLLAR

Engineered with a dense 1-inch neckband reinforced with 5% elastane memory yarn. Sits flush against the collarbone and retains its crisp circular geometry wash after wash.
