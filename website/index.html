FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Atelier Client Inquiries

Essential information regarding our garments, sizing, ordering, and delivery.

What makes 220 GSM heavyweight cotton different from standard tees? +
Standard commercial t-shirts use lightweight 140–160 GSM single jersey cotton that tends to be translucent, clingy, and easily warped after a few washes. Our 220 GSM dense interlock knit is spun from 100% combed long-staple cotton, giving the garment substantial structural weight, zero transparency, and a clean architectural drape that holds its form for years.
How should I choose my size for the intended boxy fit? +
All Shelby Hiver silhouettes are intentionally pre-tailored with an oversized, relaxed boxy cut with drop shoulders. Order your normal t-shirt size for the intended streetwear relaxed aesthetic. If you prefer a closer, athletic fit, we recommend ordering one size down. Check our interactive Size Guide for exact chest and length measurements in inches.
Will the t-shirt shrink or fade after washing? +
No. Our fabrics undergo an intensive double-preshrunk bio-enzyme wash during manufacturing before final cut and sewing. This eliminates residual fiber shrinkage and prevents post-wash distortion. For maximum longevity, wash cold inside-out and hang to dry naturally.
What is your pan-India delivery timeframe and tracking? +
All orders are processed and dispatched from our atelier within 24 hours. Metros receive delivery within 2–3 business days, and all other Indian pin codes within 3–5 business days. You will receive active tracking updates directly via SMS and WhatsApp once dispatched.
What is your 7-day size replacement guarantee? +
If your chosen size does not fit exactly how you prefer, contact our WhatsApp Concierge within 7 days of delivery. We will initiate a swift, seamless size exchange right from your doorstep at zero extra hassle.

Need Personal Styling or Sizing Advice?

Our dedicated atelier concierge is available 7 days a week on WhatsApp to assist with fit consultation, bulk inquiries, or order dispatch tracking.
