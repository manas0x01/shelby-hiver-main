Care & Wash Guide
  • Machine wash cold inside out (30°C max) to protect heavy combed fibers.
  • Do not bleach or dry clean.
  • Do not tumble dry on high heat; line dry in shade recommended.
  • Iron inside out on medium heat; avoid direct contact with monogram print.

How To Measure For Boxy Streetwear:

  • Chest: Measure across the fullest part of your chest, keeping tape horizontal under arms.
  • Length: Measure from the highest point of the shoulder down to the bottom hem.
  • Shoulder: Measure straight across from one shoulder seam to the other.
💡 Fit Recommendation: Our garments are engineered with an authentic relaxed drop-shoulder cut. If you prefer a traditional standard fit rather than oversized, we recommend ordering one size down.
